## Open questions
1. For a pilot, which identities would sign in, who may record decisions for which agency, and who at OSBM may see every agency's decisions?
2. How long should raw snapshots, decisions, evaluation labels and run logs be kept, and who may delete them?
3. Who maintains each agency profile when an agency publishes a new plan (NC DHHS's was rebuilt from the 2026-2030 plan during the event), and who approves changes to scoring weights, confidence bands (High ≥ 55, Medium ≥ 45), the off-topic field descriptions and their calibration?
4. Would a pilot run in a state-managed subscription (for example one run by NCDIT or OSBM) instead of the event subscription `unc-sdis-solveathon-team14`?
5. May staff notes name partner organizations or internal plans? If so, the notes may need a higher classification than Internal.
6. Should agency staff see other agencies' matches and decisions on the Statewide page, or only OSBM?
