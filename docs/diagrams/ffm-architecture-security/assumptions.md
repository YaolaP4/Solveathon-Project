## Open questions
1. For a pilot, which identities would sign in (NC DHHS tenant or a test tenant), and who may record decisions versus only view?
2. How long should raw snapshots, decisions, evaluation labels and run logs be kept, and who may delete them?
3. Who updates `agency/priorities.json` when the next NCDHHS strategic plan replaces 2023-2025, and who approves changes to scoring weights, confidence bands (now High ≥ 55, Medium ≥ 45), the 10 off-topic field descriptions and their calibration?
4. Would a pilot run in a state-managed subscription (for example one run by NCDIT) instead of the event subscription `unc-sdis-solveathon-team14`?
5. May staff notes name partner organizations or internal plans? If so, the notes may need a higher classification than Internal.
