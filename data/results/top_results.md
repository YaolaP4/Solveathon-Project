# Top federal grant opportunities for NCDHHS
_As of 2026-09-30. Scores are 0-100 from a fixed, published formula (strategic alignment 30%, operational fit 25%, financial value 15%, deadline 15%, eligibility confidence 15%)._

## Apply now: top 10 open opportunities

### 1. Title X Family Planning Services Grants — 98/100
- **Why:** Strong fit: Title X directly funds family planning and preconception services under DPH, advancing G2.O4 reproductive and maternal health work.
- **Owner:** Division of Public Health · **Goal:** Child and Family Well-Being · **DHHS role:** lead (verified quote)
- **Deadline:** 2027-01-11 (103 days) · **Award:** $22.0M · **Cost share:** False
- **Main risk:** The actual amount available will not be determined until enactment of the FY 2027 federal budget.
- [Grant listing](https://simpler.grants.gov/opportunity/770eae58-b245-4431-a4b8-7b1aca9e917f)

### 2. Public Health Crisis Response Cooperative Agreement — 83/100
- **Why:** Aligns with public health workforce surge staffing and DPH preparedness role; only creates an unfunded roster until CDC activates an emergency.
- **Owner:** Division of Public Health · **Goal:** Strong and Inclusive Workforce · **DHHS role:** lead (verified quote)
- **Deadline:** 2027-02-11 (134 days) · **Award:** $5.0M · **Cost share:** False
- **Main risk:** This NOFO only creates an approved but unfunded roster; actual funding is contingent on CDC determining a public health emergency exists and on future appropriations, so it may never result in award money.
- [Grant listing](https://simpler.grants.gov/opportunity/42a1b238-2c19-47b4-ae58-f4a775231b78)

### 3. Home Study and Post-Release Services for Unaccompanied Alien Children​​ — 81/100
- **Why:** Fits G2.O2 child safety and permanency and state child welfare expertise in DSS, but serves a federal immigration-specific population with high ORR compliance demands.
- **Owner:** Division of Social Services · **Goal:** Child and Family Well-Being · **DHHS role:** lead (verified quote)
- **Deadline:** 2027-10-01 (366 days) · **Award:** $100.0M · **Cost share:** False
- **Main risk:** NCDHHS does not have routine jurisdiction over unaccompanied alien children, and this ORR program is typically operated by nonprofits with immigration case management experience, making it an unfamiliar compliance-heavy program rather than a core state child welfare function.
- [Grant listing](https://simpler.grants.gov/opportunity/cb2cbb9f-741d-4e6b-ad32-557d751f9903)

### 4. Emerging Infections Network - Research for Preventing, Detecting, and Managing Travel-associated Infectious Diseases — 77/100
- **Why:** Strong fit for DPH disease surveillance and preparedness; CDC travel-focused sentinel network advances data-driven decision-making, though it is not squarely in NCDHHS strategic goals.
- **Owner:** Division of Public Health · **Goal:** Operational Excellence · **DHHS role:** lead (verified quote)
- **Deadline:** 2026-11-05 (36 days) · **Award:** $6.0M · **Cost share:** False
- **Main risk:** The international travel focus may exceed DPH's routine state-level disease surveillance authority and require coordination with federal quarantine stations, airports, or academic partners.
- [Grant listing](https://simpler.grants.gov/opportunity/10f4a697-e6a1-454a-bb73-74bc933f364a)

### 5. NARMS Cooperative Agreement Program to Strengthen Antibiotic Resistance Surveillance in Retail Food Specimens — 70/100
- **Why:** Funds antibiotic resistance surveillance in retail food; fits DPH lab and epidemiology role but is narrow and not a named NCDHHS objective.
- **Owner:** Division of Public Health · **Goal:** Operational Excellence · **DHHS role:** lead (verified quote)
- **Deadline:** 2027-04-01 (183 days) · **Award:** $200K · **Cost share:** False
- **Main risk:** Specialized FDA/NARMS retail food surveillance may require existing laboratory capacity and federal collaboration, so if NC is not already a NARMS retail food site the start-up effort could be high relative to the grant's narrow scope.
- [Grant listing](https://simpler.grants.gov/opportunity/0b08c799-a202-45f2-92ad-318044afbc2d)

### 6. Laboratory Flexible Funding Model (LFFM) — 68/100

- **Owner:** Division of Public Health · **Goal:** none · **DHHS role:** lead (verified quote)
- **Deadline:** 2028-01-11 (468 days) · **Award:** $1.5M · **Cost share:** False
